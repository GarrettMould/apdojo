QUESTION:
The small size of the several states, and the nature of their governments, would render them unfit for the national government. . . . The people of America have been taught by experience but not by theory, that the powers of the federal government are too limited. —Alexander Hamilton, 1780

Based on the text, which of the following statements would the author most likely support?

OPTIONS:
  A) The creation of a unicameral legislature where each state has one vote.
  B) The strengthening of the central government to address national concerns. ✓
  C) The preservation of the Articles of Confederation with minor amendments.
  D) The expansion of reserved powers to ensure state sovereignty.

CORRECT: B

EXPLANATION:
Hamilton argues that state governments are "unfit" for national governance and that federal powers are "too limited." This aligns with the Federalist position that a stronger central government was necessary. (A) describes the Articles of Confederation's structure, which Hamilton critiqued. (C) and (D) are Anti-Federalist leanings.

QUESTION:
In Federalist No. 10, James Madison argues that the "mischiefs of faction" can be controlled by:

OPTIONS:
  A) Removing the liberty of citizens to form interest groups.
  B) Creating a direct democracy where every citizen votes on every issue.
  C) A large republic that filters public views through elected representatives. ✓
  D) Ensuring an equal distribution of property among all citizens.

CORRECT: C

EXPLANATION:
Madison argues that you cannot remove the causes of faction (liberty) without destroying democracy; instead, you must control the effects. A large republic makes it difficult for a unified majority faction to form. (B) is rejected by Madison as prone to "turbulence." (D) is seen as impossible by Madison.

QUESTION:
Which of the following scenarios best illustrates the concept of a "concurrent power" under the U.S. Constitution?

OPTIONS:
  A) The federal government negotiates a trade treaty with China while California passes a law regulating local businesses.
  B) The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) collects income taxes at the same time the state of Ohio collects a state sales tax. ✓
  C) The President sends troops to a foreign conflict, and the Supreme Court rules on the constitutionality of a law.
  D) The state of Florida conducts a primary election while the federal government coins new currency.

CORRECT: B

EXPLANATION:
Concurrent powers are powers shared by both the state and federal governments. Taxation is the most common example. (A) and (D) involve exclusive powers (treaties and coining money are federal). (C) involves separation of powers between branches.
