# Missing Unit 5 AP Microeconomics Key Terms

Based on analysis of Unit 5 questions, here are terms that appear frequently but are missing from apMicroTerms.ts:

## High Priority (Appear in multiple questions):

1. **Marginal Product of Labor (MPL)**
   - Appears in: Questions 114, 115, 118
   - Definition needed: The additional output produced by hiring one more unit of labor

2. **Wage Rate / Wage**
   - Appears in: Questions 115, 120, 121, 122
   - Definition needed: The price paid per unit of labor

3. **Average Product of Labor (APL)**
   - Appears in: Question 115
   - Definition needed: Total product divided by the quantity of labor

4. **Total Product**
   - Appears in: Question 118
   - Definition needed: The total output produced with a given quantity of inputs

5. **Marginal Product per Dollar Spent**
   - Appears in: Question 118
   - Definition needed: The marginal product divided by the price of the factor

6. **Price of Capital**
   - Appears in: Question 117
   - Definition needed: The cost of using capital as a factor of production

7. **Substitute Factor**
   - Appears in: Question 117
   - Definition needed: Factors of production that can be used in place of each other

8. **Labor Productivity**
   - Appears in: Question 116
   - Definition needed: The amount of output produced per unit of labor

9. **Wage Taker**
   - Appears in: Question 115 (implied)
   - Definition needed: A firm that cannot influence the market wage rate

10. **Price Taker in the Labor Market**
    - Appears in: Question 120
    - Definition needed: A firm that accepts the market wage rate as given

11. **Marginal Resource Cost (MRC)**
    - Appears in: Mentioned as alternative to MFC
    - Definition needed: Same as MFC, alternative terminology

12. **Perfectly Competitive Product Market**
    - Appears in: Question 115
    - Definition needed: A product market where firms are price takers

13. **Perfectly Competitive Factor Market**
    - Appears in: Question 115
    - Definition needed: A factor market where firms are price takers

14. **Immigration**
    - Appears in: Question 117
    - Definition needed: Movement of people into a country, affecting labor supply

15. **Population Growth**
    - Appears in: Question 117
    - Definition needed: Increase in population, affecting labor supply
